What is a UTM checker?

A UTM checker reviews campaign URLs for missing, inconsistent, or risky tracking parameters before they are shared.

Required campaign parameters

  • utm_source identifies where traffic comes from.
  • utm_medium identifies the marketing channel.
  • utm_campaign identifies the campaign.

Why tracking QA matters

A URL can be technically valid while still creating fragmented reports. Consistent lowercase naming, complete required fields, and privacy-safe campaign values make handoff easier for marketers, analysts, and developers.
